DOMAIN: WATER MANAGEMENT

TOPIC: Elevated Water Consumption

When water consumption increases unexpectedly, facility operators should
first verify the sensor reading and investigate the affected building.

Potential causes include:
- Leakage
- Increased occupancy
- Excessive restroom usage
- Irrigation demand
- Cleaning operations
- Faulty fixtures
- Abnormal equipment consumption

Recommended investigation sequence:

1. Verify the sensor reading.
2. Compare consumption with the recent baseline.
3. Check occupancy and facility activity.
4. Inspect high-consumption areas.
5. Check for leakage.
6. Inspect water fixtures and equipment.
7. Review irrigation and cleaning schedules.

If elevated consumption persists, investigate the affected zone and
consider maintenance action.

Water recommendations should consider current consumption, historical
baseline, occupancy, anomaly status, and operational context.
